Compilation MPI:

mpicc -g -Wall file_name.c -o file_name

Execution MPI:

mpiexec -n <number of processes> ./file_name


Compilation OpenMP:

gcc -g -Wall -fopenmp file_name.c -o file_name

Execution OpenMP:

OMP_NUM_THREADS=<number of threads> ./file_name


Debugging MPI:

Eseguire con un solo processo:

mpiexec -n 1 ./file_name


Debugging OpenMP:

Eseguire con un solo thread:

OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 ./file_name
